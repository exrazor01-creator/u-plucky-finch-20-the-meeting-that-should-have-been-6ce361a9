The Meeting That Should Have Been an Email — one line that makes a meeting-that-should-have-been-an-email funny, judged two at a time.

## The entry

> We spent an hour in a meeting to decide the meeting about the meeting, then emailed everyone the decision we made in the first five minutes.

140 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–200 character gate.

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **On subject** — one joke about a meeting that should have been an email, plainly and unmistakably.
2. **The laugh lands on its own** — the turn arrives in the last words: the decision was made in the first five minutes and went out by email anyway.
3. **Surprise** — a meeting called to decide the meeting about the meeting, a recursion everyone in an office has lived through, not the obvious complaint about how long it ran.
4. **Economy** — every word earns its place; no throat-clearing, no second sentence.
5. **Recognition** — the meeting eating itself is something people have actually sat through, so the laugh comes with a nod.
